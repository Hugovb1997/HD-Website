The Methodology

Why Most Outbound
Fails By Design

Every agency you've tried did the same thing.

Curate a list. Filter by job title and company size. Layer on "intent signals" — funding rounds, new hires, tech stack changes — and start blasting.

These signals don't signal pain. They signal activity.

A company that just raised funding isn't automatically a buyer. A company hiring a marketing director isn't automatically looking for help. But every outbound agency builds their entire strategy on this assumption.

Same signals. Same people. Same messages.

Now, what if instead of guessing who might buy — you could actually prove it?
